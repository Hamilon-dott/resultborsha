GPA Calculation & 4th Subject Rule

Total GPA is calculated by averaging grade points earned in mandatory subjects. If a student earns above 2.00 points in their 4th (optional) subject, points above 2.00 are added as a bonus to boost overall GPA up to a maximum cap of 5.00!

Board Challenge, Re-scrutiny & Important Risks 2026

If a candidate believes their exam paper was evaluated unfairly or marks were misplaced, they can apply for official paper re-scrutiny (খাতা পুনর্নিরীক্ষণ / বোর্ড চ্যালেঞ্জ) within 7 days of result publication in 2026.

Verify Original Marksheet First

Ensure you have downloaded your full subject-wise marksheet before applying.

Crucial Facts & Re-checking Risks

What Happens During Re-check: Under Education Board rules, examiners do NOT re-read or re-grade written answers. Re-scrutiny ONLY verifies three critical points:
  • Whether all written answers received marks on the inner pages.
  • Whether marks were correctly transferred from inner pages to the cover page.
  • Whether the total sum of marks on the cover page was mathematically calculated without errors.
  • Mark Revision Outcome: Your marks can either increase or remain unchanged. Board regulations state that awarded marks will never be decreased during re-scrutiny.
  • Application Fee: The application fee (approx. TK 125 to 150 per subject paper) is strictly non-refundable regardless of whether your grade changes or remains the same.

Re-scrutiny Application Process via Teletalk SMS

  1. Applications are accepted exclusively via Teletalk Prepaid SIM.
  2. Type SMS: RSC <Board Code> <Roll No> <Subject Code> and send to 16222.
  3. You will receive a PIN number in reply with fee details.
  4. To confirm: Type RSC YES <PIN> <Contact Mobile No> and send to 16222.
